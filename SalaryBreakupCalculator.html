How Gratuity Is Calculated

Gratuity is a statutory benefit under the Payment of Gratuity Act — your employer sets aside a share of your Basic every year so it's ready to pay out when you leave after 5+ years of service.

Assumptions: Basic (50% of CTC), HRA (74% of Basic), Cell & Internet Allowance, Special Allowance and Wellness Reimbursement are all manual inputs above — Gross Salary is simply their sum, so it moves as you edit any of them. "Total CTC" is computed by adding Employer PF and Gratuity back on top of that Gross Salary, so it reflects what you've entered rather than a fixed target — adjust any field to match your own company's structure. Employer PF and Gratuity are treated as part of CTC (added on top of Gross Salary, not deducted from it). A one-time Bonus is added to taxable income for the year but excluded from the recurring monthly take-home. Income tax uses FY 2025-26 New Tax Regime slabs with ₹75,000 standard deduction and Section 87A rebate (nil tax up to ₹12L taxable income); marginal relief just above ₹12L is not modeled, and real payroll often spreads TDS unevenly across months (e.g. more in the month a bonus is paid) rather than the flat monthly average shown here. Professional tax varies by state — adjust the monthly value above. This is a template for estimation, not a payroll or tax filing tool.
